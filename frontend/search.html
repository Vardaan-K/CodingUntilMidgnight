HOW IT WORKS
01
Search

Enter any business name. We look it up across public records, review platforms, and news archives.

02
Gather

Our AI reads thousands of signals — Glassdoor, Yelp, Reddit, court filings, health inspections, and more.

03
Score

Every signal is weighted by credibility, recency, and severity. The result is a 0–100 Whistleblower Score.

04
Trace

Every score links back to its source. No black boxes — every point can be traced to a public receipt.

ABOUT

Accountability through
public signals.

Whistleblower is an AI-powered platform that aggregates public records, employee reviews, health inspections, news reports, and social signals to produce a single, traceable accountability score for any business.

We don't take sides. Every point in a score is backed by a public source anyone can read. No anonymous accusations, no black boxes — just the signal, weighted by recency and credibility.

WHAT WE TRACK
  • Employee reviews & labor complaints
  • Health & safety inspection records
  • Customer discrimination reports
  • Local news & court filings
  • Reddit, Yelp, Google, Glassdoor, Indeed
